# Gesturely

Trackpad gestures, simplified — swipe, tap, or draw a shape to move windows,
launch apps, and automate your Mac. Requires macOS 14 or later.

## Download

Go to [**Releases**](https://github.com/hardik-chaudhari7797/gesturely-releases/releases/latest)
and download the `.zip` under **Assets** on the latest release.

## Install

1. Unzip the download.
2. Drag **Gesturely.app** into your **Applications** folder. (Don't leave
   it running from Desktop, Documents, or Downloads — macOS restricts
   access to those folders by default, which silently breaks automatic
   updates. Applications is the right home for it.)

## First launch — one extra step

Gesturely isn't distributed through the Mac App Store, so the first time
you open it, macOS will warn that it's from an "unidentified developer" and
refuse to open normally. This is expected, and only happens once:

1. **Right-click** (or Control-click) **Gesturely.app** in Applications.
2. Choose **Open** from the menu.
3. A dialog appears warning you again — click **Open** on that dialog too.

After that, Gesturely opens normally every time, including after automatic
updates — you'll never see this warning again for this app.

## After that

Gesturely will walk you through the rest on first launch: granting
Accessibility permission (needed for it to actually move windows) and a
quick tour of the built-in gestures. It checks for updates automatically in
the background, or you can trigger a check anytime from its menu bar icon.
